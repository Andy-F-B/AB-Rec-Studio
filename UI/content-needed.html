Home

Mostly fills itself from the other pages. You only need:

Studio taglineOne line under the studio name.
e.g. Record, mix and master with people who love music.
Which musicians are featuredUp to 4 shown on the home page.
e.g. Featured: yes / no for each musician

Musicians

content/musicians.ts

For each musician you have worked with (as many as you like):

Name
e.g. Jane Doe Band
GenreUsed for the filter buttons.
e.g. Indie Rock
Short bio1 sentence for the card.
e.g. Four-piece band who tracked their debut album live with us.
PhotoSquare-ish, at least 800 × 800 px. Save as public/images/musicians/<name>.jpg
e.g. jane-doe-band.jpg

Musician profile

Extra details per musician (all optional except bio):

Full bioA few short paragraphs.
LinksWebsite, Spotify, Instagram, YouTube. Leave out any they don’t have.
e.g. https://open.spotify.com/artist/…
CreditsWhat you made together: title, year, and recording / mixing / mastering / production. Optional link.
e.g. “Night Drive” EP, 2025, mixing

Festival

content/festival.ts

Everything about the festival:

Festival name
e.g. AB Studio Fest 2027
Date(s)Start date, and end date if more than one day. Currently a guess: June 12, 2027.
e.g. 2027-06-12 to 2027-06-13
Time zoneSo lineup times show correctly.
e.g. America/New_York
Short tagline + description1 line, then a short paragraph.
VenueName and full address (powers the Maps link).
e.g. Riverside Park Bandshell, 12 River Rd, …
LineupEach act: name, optional stage, optional start time. Say which ones are studio musicians so they link to their profile.
e.g. Jane Doe Band, Main Stage, 6:00 pm
FAQ answersAge limit? Parking / transport? Any other questions you want answered.
e.g. All ages. Free parking on River Rd.
Hero photo (optional)Wide, at least 1600 px. public/images/festival/hero.jpg
